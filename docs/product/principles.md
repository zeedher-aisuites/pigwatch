# Product Principles

## Purpose

PigWatch helps farm operators and veterinary professionals notice physiological and behavioral anomalies earlier by combining observations across sensors and time.

## Safety statement

PigWatch is decision support. It does not independently diagnose veterinary disease, prescribe treatment, or replace professional evaluation. Product language must communicate evidence, confidence, uncertainty, and escalation paths.

## Development principles

1. Simulation is a supported operating mode from the first implementation of a capability.
2. Simulated, recorded, and live adapters implement stable capability contracts.
3. Ground truth is kept separate from observed state.
4. Raw observations remain traceable through transformations and analysis.
5. Large dependencies and infrastructure are introduced only with a demonstrated milestone need.
